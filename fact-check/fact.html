HOW TO USE THESE TOOLS

A simple guide to fact-check information online.

1

STOP

Don't immediately believe or share the information.

2

CHECK THE SOURCE

Who published it and are they credible?

3

LOOK AT THE EVIDENCE

Check the evidence, data, or references provided.

4

CROSS-CHECK

Compare with other reliable sources.

5

CHECK THE CONTEXT

Is the information current and in the right context?
