What is Digital Manipulation?

Digital manipulation is the alteration of a photo, video, or audio file using editing software, cropping, splicing, retouching, or Artificial Intelligence to change what it originally showed.

Reference: https://cloud.google.com/learn/what-is-artificial-intelligence
02

Concerns About Digital AI Manipulation

False Narratives

AI-manipulated media can spread false stories about events that never actually happened.

Reputation Damage

People may appear in fabricated images or videos that can negatively affect their reputation.

Loss of Trust

Constant exposure to manipulated media can reduce public trust in legitimate photos, videos, and online information.

Reference: https://www.ibm.com/think/insights/10-ai-dangers-and-risks-and-how-to-manage-them

03

How to Detect Digital AI Manipulation

Lighting & Shadows

Check whether the lighting direction and shadows are consistent throughout the image.

Reverse Image Search

Search the image online to determine whether an original version already exists.

Metadata (EXIF)

Inspect image metadata for editing software or AI generation traces.

Repeated Patterns

Look closely for blurred edges, repeated textures, or unnatural facial symmetry.

Reference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBOJJlP68qo

AI Image Detection Tools

Hive Moderation

A free AI detector that performs an initial analysis of images and identifies AI-generated content.

Illuminarty

Uses visual heatmaps to explain which image regions appear to be AI generated.

Reverse Image Search

Compare the uploaded image with earlier versions found across the internet.

Metadata Checker

Examine EXIF information to determine editing history and file origin.
