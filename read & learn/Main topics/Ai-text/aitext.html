What is AI Generated Misinformation?

AI-generated misinformation is false or misleading content, including text, images, videos, or audio, that is created or amplified using Artificial Intelligence tools, sometimes intentionally to deceive people.

Reference: https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/artificial-intelligence
02

Concerns About AI Generated Misinformation

Fast & Low-Cost Creation

AI makes it cheaper, faster, and easier to create fake text, images, videos, and audio that can spread rapidly across digital platforms.

Public Manipulation

AI-generated misinformation can manipulate public perception, damage reputations, and even influence financial markets before the truth is verified.

Real-World Impact

In May 2023, a fake AI-generated image showing an explosion near the Pentagon spread on social media and briefly caused stock prices to decline before it was debunked.

Reference: https://www.resolver.com/blog/ai-generated-misinformation-brand-risks/

How to Detect AI Generated Misinformation

Verify the Source

Check whether the information comes from a credible, traceable, and trustworthy source before believing or sharing it.

Watch for Unnatural Writing

AI-generated misinformation may contain awkward, repetitive, or unusually polished wording that feels unnatural.

Cross-Check the Claim

See whether independent and reputable news organizations have already reported or debunked the same claim.

Verify Before Sharing

If reliable evidence cannot confirm the claim, avoid sharing the content until it has been properly verified.

Reference: https://www.techtarget.com/searchenterpriseai/feature/How-to-detect-AI-generated-content
04

Tools to Detect AI-Made Images

Hive Moderation

Upload an image to receive a probability score indicating whether it was generated using Artificial Intelligence.

Optic AI or Not

A drag-and-drop detector that estimates whether an image is AI-generated and may identify the model used to create it.

Illuminarty

Detects AI-generated images, audio, and text using machine learning analysis.

SynthID

Google's invisible watermark technology that helps identify supported AI-generated content.
