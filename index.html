ABOUT CREDIVAULT

Learn More About CrediVault

A digital learning platform for evaluating the credibility of social media news.

What is CrediVault?

CrediVault is a web-based interactive learning platform designed to enhance ICT students' ability to evaluate social media news. It provides learning materials, interactive activities, verification resources, and guided tools that build the skills needed to critically examine information before accepting or sharing it.

What its purpose?

CrediVault aims to strengthen students' ability to recognize and evaluate potentially misleading information online. It encourages users to examine sources, consider available evidence, compare information, and verify claims before deciding whether information is credible enough to believe or share.

What does CrediVault offer?

  • Learning content on social media information credibility
  • Interactive activities for applying credibility-checking skills
  • Lessons on AI-generated content, clickbait, popularity, and cross-checking
  • Verification and fact-checking resources
  • CrediBot for guided questions about online information and verification
