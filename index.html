BEFORE YOU SHARE

Check Before You Share

Not everything you see online is automatically reliable. Take a moment to examine the information before believing or sharing it.

01

Stop

Pause before reacting or sharing.

02

Check

Look at the source and supporting evidence.

03

Verify

Compare the information with trusted sources.

QUICK CHECK

Quick Credibility Check

Ask yourself these simple questions before trusting information online.

Who posted it?

Check who created or published the information.

What is the evidence?

Look for reliable evidence supporting the claim.

Does another source confirm it?

Compare the claim with other trusted sources.

Does it feel misleading?

Watch for exaggerated, emotional, or suspicious claims.
