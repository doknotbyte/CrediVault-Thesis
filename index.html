ABOUT CREDIVAULT

Your Guide to Credible Information

CrediVault is a web-based interactive learning platform designed to help ICT students develop the knowledge and skills needed to evaluate the credibility of information encountered on social media.

Through interactive lessons, activities, verification tools, and guided learning experiences, CrediVault helps users become more careful and critical when consuming and sharing online information.

ABOUT CREDIVAULT

Learn More About CrediVault

A digital learning space for evaluating the credibility of social media information.

What is CrediVault?

CrediVault is a web-based interactive learning platform focused on helping ICT students evaluate the credibility of information found on social media. It provides lessons, activities, and verification resources that encourage users to think critically before believing or sharing online information.

What is its purpose?

CrediVault aims to strengthen users' ability to recognize misleading information, examine online content carefully, and verify claims using reliable sources before accepting or sharing them.

What does CrediVault offer?

  • Interactive learning lessons
  • Interactive activities and challenges
  • Fact-checking and verification resources
  • CrediBot for guided questions
  • Practical tips for evaluating social media information
